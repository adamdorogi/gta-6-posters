# Website surfaces

The landing page helps prospective buyers select a style and upload a photo. The generation preview lets them inspect a full poster, edit it, and compare purchase options. Keep the existing copy and scrolling carousels.

## Direction contract

THESIS. A tropical game launch framed as a working poster studio. Original art shows what the uploader can make.

OWNED FORM. Illustrated cover panels move in opposite directions against a coastal skyline. Condensed poster type and quiet controls organize the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT. The full headline, three visual style choices, and the upload target fit beside the moving poster wall. Each option shows its own illustration.

VISITOR PATH. A poster sample leads to style selection, upload, and the full preview. Purchase options sit beside the art on wide screens.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION. Keep the cover panels moving during normal browsing. Give the separate showcase touch scrolling and keyboard-accessible controls.

CROSS-SURFACE REACH. Dark plum, pale peach, and blush carry through the preview, editor, product details, and footer. Narrow screens give each action and artwork room in sequence.

Design direction reference 182d4856. The user supplied the game’s campaign page and delegated styling choices.
