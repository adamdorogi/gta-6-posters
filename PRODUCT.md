# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People creating illustrated posters from uploaded portraits, including photos of themselves, groups, or pets.

## Product Purpose

Visitors select a style, upload photos, and receive a generated preview. They can revise the design before deciding whether to order a print or digital download.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The catalog has three GTA 6-inspired styles. Likeness to the uploaded subjects is the first priority. New artwork uses portrait A-series proportions. The store ships glossy, unframed A4, A3, A2, and A1 paper posters internationally and charges in USD.

## Brand Commitments

The visual direction draws on the Rockstar GTA VI site. The scrolling carousels show artwork across all three styles. The public website is gta6posters.com and customer emails use hello@gta6posters.com.

## Evidence on Hand

The landing page contains the creator, print mockup, showcase, and FAQ. The user provided an existing generation preview to verify the edit and purchase layout.
